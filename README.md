# Vertical farming room: equipment sizing worksheet

Planning worksheet: from the floor area of a room to towers, planting ports, weekly plants, nursery trays and pump flow. Floor per tower, ports and plant weight are **planning assumptions to replace with the tower you are quoted and your own crop records**.

## Assumptions used

40% of the floor kept for aisles, plant room and nursery; 0.9 m2 per tower; 28 ports per tower; 35-day crop; 330 working days; 8% seedling loss; 98-cavity trays; 150 g a plant; 30 L/h per tower.

## The five equipment blocks

1. Growing system: vertical towers, pyramid NFT channels or raft beds.
2. Pumps and reservoir that feed it.
3. Nursery that supplies seedlings.
4. Growing media.
5. Controls and spares.

Choose the growing system by crop and floor; the number that drives all five blocks is how many plants must come out each week.
